This book was selected by Magnolia Members and will be read from May 4 - June 14.

The branch member who nominated the book has a few words to share on why they made this suggestion:

"Giovanni’s Room was on my tbr for years and when I finally read it last year I loved it. It’s heartbreaking and it’s beautifully written and although it is very short, I think there will be a lot to discuss in the branches. I’m very curious to hear what all of you think about the book."
