This book was selected by communiy vote and will be read from June 29 - August 9.

The member who nominated the book has a few words to share on why they made this suggestion:

"I love this book so much. It has great, complicated characters with intense and complex relationships, is very suspenseful, and shows a great love for Shakespeare and his relevance in the modern day. The writing is great and there’s a mystery, so I think we would have lots to discuss in the channels."
